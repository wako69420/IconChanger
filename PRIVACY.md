# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 30, 2026

## Overview
Icon Changer is a local macOS utility. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## Data Collection
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**
- All icon modifications and file operations occur strictly on your local machine.
- Downloaded images and icons are saved directly to your local file system.
- Network requests are only made to third-party icon websites (e.g., macOSIcons, Icons8) via the built-in web browser, and to GitHub to check for application updates.

## Third-Party Services
When browsing icon websites within the app, their respective privacy policies apply. Icon Changer does not intercept or track your browsing activity on these sites.
